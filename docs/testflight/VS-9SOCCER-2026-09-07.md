# CAPS vs 9SOCCER — WHY ONE REACHES THE PHONE

**The difference is not configuration. It is that the CAPS invitation was never accepted.**

`royearguan@gmail.com` is `INVITED` on CAPS and `INSTALLED` on 9Soccer. Everything else that
could have differed — the groups, their names, their settings, the build states, export
compliance — is the same or better on CAPS.

**Fixed automatically: the invitation has been re-sent** (HTTP 201). It still has to be tapped on
the phone, and that is Apple's design, not a missing feature.

⚠️ **Plugging the iPhone into the computer will not help, and it is worth knowing why.** That phone
receives 9Soccer builds today. That single fact clears the device, the cable, the Apple ID's
health, and TestFlight itself. Whatever differs sits on Apple's side, and no cable reaches it.

## §2 · Every difference found, including the ones that look irrelevant

1. **⚠️ THE TESTER STATE.** CAPS `INVITED`, 9Soccer `INSTALLED`. **This is the one that explains it.**
2. **External group attachment.** Every recent 9Soccer build reaches its external "Friends" group
   (758, 757, 756, 755, 754). CAPS's "Friends" last received **build 450** — it serves
   450, 449, 445, 327, 16. Real, but it affects external testers only, and Roye is internal.
3. **minOsVersion.** CAPS 15.1, 9Soccer 15.0. Irrelevant unless the phone is on iOS 15.0 exactly,
   which is vanishingly unlikely — but it is a difference and it is listed.
4. **Public link token.** CAPS `hD3KvZeC`, 9Soccer `ZrnsQbQd`. Cosmetic.
5. **SKU.** CAPS `CAPS1234`, 9Soccer `ninesoccer`. Cosmetic.
6. **Group count on other apps.** Wingman has four groups, ExplainIT has two *internal* groups
   (one of them named "Friends", which is confusing but harmless). Neither is in play here.

**Everything that could have blocked CAPS is clear.** The build is VALID, unexpired, compliance
answered, `IN_BETA_TESTING`, and attached to an internal group whose `hasAccessToAllBuilds` is
true. There is nothing wrong with CAPS's configuration.

## §3 · Fixed automatically

`tools/asc/resend_invite.rb`, dispatched through the workflow:

```
BEFORE  royearguan@gmail.com  state="INVITED"  inviteType="EMAIL"
POST /v1/betaTesterInvitations -> HTTP 201
AFTER (re-read from Apple)  state="INVITED"  inviteType="EMAIL"
```

**Verified from Apple's records, not from the 201** — the script re-reads the tester after posting.

⚠️ **`state` staying `INVITED` is the correct and expected result.** It changes to `INSTALLED` only
when the invitation is accepted **on a device**. That tap is how Apple binds a tester to a device;
no API can perform it, and pretending otherwise would be the "green workflow, nothing on the phone"
failure this whole line of work exists to avoid.

**Nothing was added, removed or modified.** The tester already existed; this re-sent the email.

### What I deliberately did NOT do

⚠️ **I did not attach build 515 to the external "Friends" group**, even though that is the one
structural difference from 9Soccer and the brief invited me to match it. Attaching a build to an
external group puts it into **Beta App Review**, and the brief says not to submit anything for
review. 9Soccer's external group serves current builds because it has been through that review;
CAPS's has not. That is a real, available improvement and it is Roye's call, not mine.

**9soccer untouched:** confirmed. Every 9Soccer call in this sprint was a `GET`. The only writes
anywhere were one `POST /v1/betaTesterInvitations` against **CAPS**, and commits to this repo.

## §4 · The shortest path, ranked

### 1. Accept the invitation — the real fix, about a minute

A fresh invitation email is in `royearguan@gmail.com` now.

* On the second phone, install **TestFlight** from the App Store if it is not already there.
* Open the invitation email **on that phone** and tap **View in TestFlight**.
* **What he sees:** TestFlight opens, CAPS appears with an **INSTALL** button, and it installs
  **build 515** — the internal group has access to all builds.

### 2. If the email does not arrive — redeem instead

Open TestFlight → top right → **Redeem**. The code is in the invitation email.

### 3. The public link — ⚠️ only as a last resort, and it serves a stale build

`https://testflight.apple.com/join/hD3KvZeC` works on **any** Apple ID with no invitation.
**But that link belongs to the external "Friends" group, and Friends currently serves build 450**,
not 515. He would install an app from months ago, missing everything from the last five days.
Use only if routes 1 and 2 both fail, and expect an old build.

### If it still does not appear

1. **Check the Apple ID on that phone:** Settings → tap the name at the top → the email beneath.
   If it is not `royearguan@gmail.com`, that address needs adding — dispatch `testflight-manage.yml`
   with `action: add-tester` and that email.
2. **Check TestFlight is actually installed** on that phone. It is a separate app from the App Store.
3. **Check the iOS version** is 15.1 or later.
4. **Pull to refresh** inside TestFlight — its list caches.
