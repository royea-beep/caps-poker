## THE SESSION — מה קרה

### ⏱️ דקה 0-2: First Impressions

**Negreanu** (מסתכל על המסך): "Wait — all four cards play? No choosing two? That's... actually huge. In Omaha you're always thinking 'which two do I use.' Here you're thinking 'WHERE do I put them.' That's a completely different skill tree."

**Hellmuth** (מעיף מבט): "The board layout... I need to see my cards bigger. What am I looking at, cards for ants?"

**Ivey** (שקט. 10 שניות. אז): "I like the timer. Forces decisions."

**Mizrachi**: "Four boards in heads up? That's like playing four Omaha tables at once but they're connected. My brain is already working."

### ⏱️ דקה 3-15: First Hands (1v1)

**Negreanu vs Mizrachi — Hand 1:**
Daniel deals his 16 cards. Stares for 40 seconds.

**Negreanu**: "OK so the meta here is — do I stack one board to guarantee a win, or spread strength evenly? Because if I sweep all boards I get the COMPLETE bonus... 50% extra... that changes EVERYTHING about allocation strategy."

**Mizrachi** (grinning): "I just put my best Omaha hands on boards 1 and 4, and sacrificed 2 and 3. Let's see."

Result: Mizrachi wins 3/4 boards. Negreanu gets zero COMPLETE.

**Negreanu**: "See! He read that I was going for the sweep and countered by conceding two boards. There's a whole Nash Equilibrium thing here that doesn't exist in Hold'em."

**Hellmuth** (watching): "But I can't SEE the cards. They're too small. And why is the community flop already open? In regular poker the suspense is—"

**Ivey**: "Phil. The flop is open ON PURPOSE. You need to see the flop to decide where to put your cards. That IS the game."

**Hellmuth**: "...fine. But the cards are still too small."

### ⏱️ דקה 15-30: Getting Into It

**Bonomo** (from Table 2, walks over): "I've played 6 hands and I already see three distinct strategies: stack-one-board, spread-even, and read-and-counter. This has GTO depth."

**Kornuth**: "The COMPLETE mechanic is genius for a product. It's a comeback mechanism AND a gambling accelerator. People will chase COMPLETE the way they chase flushes."

**Rampage** (recording on phone): "Chat, this is NOT what I expected. This plays like chess meets Omaha meets fantasy football draft. The allocation phase is where all the skill is."

**Seidel** (quietly to Ivey): "The turn and river being hidden is perfect. You commit to a board blind — just like real poker. The reveal sequence creates natural drama."

**Ivey** nods.

### ⏱️ דקה 30-45: The Bugs & Issues

**Hellmuth** (slams table): "WHY can't I zoom in on a board? I tap a board and nothing happens. I need to SEE the cards!"

**Ali Imsirovic**: "On the 3-player game — the third player's cards are overlapping the board border. And when the reveal hits board 2 and 3 simultaneously, I lose track of which one I won."

**Ivey**: "The chip animation is wrong. When I win a COMPLETE, the 50% bonus chips appear but there's no visual distinction from the regular pot. It should feel different. Bigger. Special."

**Bonomo**: "The timer sound is the same for 30 seconds left and 5 seconds left. Needs urgency scaling."

**Kornuth**: "The 'Ready' button after card placement — it's too small and too close to the board. I almost fat-fingered it twice."

**Rampage**: "The summary screen is... boring. After all that drama in the game, you show me a plain score table? Where's the highlights? Show me my best hand. Show me the COMPLETE moment replayed."

**Negreanu**: "One more thing — there's no way to preview what hand I have on a board before I commit. If I put 4 cards on board 1, I want to see 'Two Pair' or 'Flush Draw' before I press Ready."

### ⏱️ דקה 45-60: The Verdict Discussion

**Negreanu** (stands up): "OK let me be real with you guys. This game has something. The allocation phase is the most original poker mechanic I've seen since PLO got popular. The question is whether casual players will get it."

**Hellmuth**: "Casual players LOVE this. You know why? Because even if you're bad at poker, you might accidentally stack a board well. The variance is higher than Hold'em but the skill ceiling is ALSO higher. That's rare."

**Mizrachi**: "I want to play this for money. Right now. That tells you everything."

**Ivey**: "The game itself — 9 out of 10. The app — 6. Fix the card size, fix the reveal sequence clarity, fix the summary screen. Then it's ready."

**Seidel**: "I've seen 50 poker variants in my career. Most die because they don't have a 'hook.' COMPLETE is the hook. People will TALK about the time they swept all 4 boards."

**Bryn Kenney**: "From a business angle — this is a mobile game first, poker second. The 90-second timer makes it perfect for toilet gaming. The COMPLETE bonus makes people want 'one more game.' That's the addiction loop."

**Rampage**: "I would stream this TODAY if the UI was tighter. My viewers would go insane for the reveal sequence. It's like opening loot boxes but with skill."
