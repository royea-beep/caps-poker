## §2 · STILL LIVE ON 515 — ranked by what it costs a player

### 1. The rank is the smallest thing on the card, and the suit is drawn twice

**The dominant cluster, and the only sustained player feedback this project owns.** Between
2026-03-21 and 2026-03-28 one tester filed the same request eleven times — reports 9, 22, 70, 72,
73, 74, 75, 77, 78, 81 and the MASTER (#7, itself titled *"Card readability overhaul — 7+ reports
same issue"*) — then again as a 1-star in May (#713, *"the cards are too small, I can't see anything
on my device"*). What he asked for, every time:

> remove the small rank and suit in the top-left corner — leave only the big rank and the big suit
> in the middle — then the cards can shrink and everything fits

**Measured on the built app** (`tests/card-corner-probe.mjs`, 393×852, practice, 2 players). A
face-up card is **40 × 56 px** and carries **exactly three glyphs**:

| glyph | size | where |
|---|---:|---|
| rank (`5`, `10`, `A`) | **12 px** | top-left corner |
| suit | 9 px | under the rank |
| suit | **26 px** | dead centre |

At 3 players the cards are 46 px and the rank is 14 px. **There is no large centre rank at either
count — 0 centre rank glyphs measured.** `Card.tsx` still computes `centerRankSize` (:248) and
still defines a `centerRankText` style (:679); neither is used by the render. They are dead.

⚠️ **The app did the opposite of the request on both halves.** The tester wanted the corner index
gone and the big centre rank kept. 515 keeps the corner index, **adds a second one** (bottom-right,
rotated 180°), and **deletes the centre rank**. `Card.tsx:23` says so out loud: *"CORNER GLYPHS
DELIBERATELY UNTOUCHED."*

**What it costs a player:** to arrange 16 cards across 4 boards under a clock, the one datum you
must read is the rank. It is rendered at 12 px, in the corner, and **nowhere else** — while 26 px,
more than twice the size, is spent repeating a suit that is already shown 9 px above it.

This is **not a bug and not mine to fix.** The corner index is the real playing-card convention and
the tester's ask is unusual; deleting it is a design decision. But it is the decision he asked to
have reversed twelve times and nobody ever answered him. **Roye's call.**

*Correction found while measuring:* `Card.tsx:25` says the bottom-right index renders at
`width >= 54` so *"3P/4P yes, 2P no"*. Measured **0 bottom-right indices at BOTH counts** — 3P cards
are 46 px on a 393 pt phone, under the gate. The comment is wrong about 3P.

Evidence: `docs/read-the-204/card-corner-probe.json`, `one-card-2p.png`, `cards-2p.png`, `cards-3p.png`.

### 2. The bug reporter calls itself a video reporter and has never captured a video

⚠️ **This is the one that matters for the tester round, because it is the round's own evidence channel.**

Proven from the data, not the code:

| measured across all 252 rows | |
|---|---:|
| rows with a `video_url` | 29 |
| of those, pointing at `/frames/bug-frame-*.jpg` | **29** |
| of those, an actual `.mp4` / `.mov` / `.webm` | **0** |
| of those, where `video_url` **equals** `screenshot_url` | **29** |

The code says why. `handleSend` uploads **one still frame** and writes that single URL into both
columns:

```js
const videoUrl = lastFrame ? await withTimeout(uploadFrame(lastFrame), 5000, null, 'upload-frame') : null;
...  audioUrl, videoUrl, screenshotUrl: videoUrl,
```

`uploadFrame` writes `frames/bug-frame-*.jpg` with `contentType: 'image/jpeg'`. The recorder
(`utils/screenRecorder.ts`) is a **0.5 fps screenshot sampler capped at `MAX_FRAMES = 10`**, and
`stopRecording()`'s return value is **discarded by a leading comma** in `handleStop`. Only
`frames[frames.length - 1]` is ever uploaded — **nine of ten captured frames are thrown away on
every report.** `report_type: 'video'` is a hardcoded string literal.

And the losses are silent, by design of the timeouts:

* **7 of 36** rows flagged `has_video: true` have **no URL at all** — the 5 s frame upload returned
  `null` and the report was filed anyway.
* **15 of 42** video reports have **no audio** — 8 s `stop-audio` plus 10 s upload, same silent null.
  Report **#15** is literally titled *"Again no audio"*.

The audio, where it survives, is genuine: 27 rows carry a real `.m4a`.

**What it costs:** a tester narrates a bug for twenty seconds and the pipeline keeps one JPEG, or
nothing. The column name promises a video that the code has never been able to produce.

### 3. Board 4 overflows the visible area by 44 px at 2 players

Reports **77** and **78** asked to shrink the cards *"so all the boards fit properly"* and

## §5 · PATTERNS — the design findings, and they are worth more than any single defect

1. **One person asked for the same thing twelve times and the product shipped the opposite.** See
   §2.1. Whatever the right answer is, *nobody ever answered him* — and he is the only tester this
   project has ever had.
2. **There is exactly one voice in the entire corpus.** 207 text reports carry **one** distinct
   `tester_name`. In six months there has never been a second person's opinion in this table.
3. **The corpus is 77% machine noise.** 195 of 252. Anyone told "252 reports exist" would badly
   overestimate how much this project knows about how it plays.
4. **Every label in this pipeline is unreliable, and always in the same direction** — a name that is
   not the content. `video_url` holding a JPEG · `report_type: 'video'` hardcoded · statuses applied
   in bulk · `ai_screen` reading "Home/Lobby" on a report about a tagline. This is the same shape as
   the catch-all 404 written into a file production never reads and the `_comment_rewrites` that
   killed 29 deploys. **It is this project's signature defect, and it is now four for four.**
