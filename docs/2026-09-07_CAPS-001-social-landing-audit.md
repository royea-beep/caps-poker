## 🔴 The headline: the finished video ends on two links that do not exist

The explainer closes on `caps.ftable.co.il` plus **App Store / Google Play**. Neither store link is
real today. CAPS is **TestFlight only** — it is not in the App Store — and there is **no Play Store
submission path configured at all**. A viewer who follows the end card reaches a game they cannot
install from either store. That is the CHICLE failure exactly: a video pointing at a shop that
cannot sell.

The one link that does work is `caps.ftable.co.il`, and it is the game itself, playable in a
browser. If the video must ship before the stores are real, that is the only end card the product
can currently back.

## 1 · Social profiles — none exist in this repository

A full-text sweep for `instagram`, `tiktok`, `facebook`, `twitter`, `x.com`, `youtube`, `threads`
and `linkedin` returns **no account, handle or profile URL anywhere**. Every hit is something else:

| what matched | what it actually is |
|---|---|
| `components/ShareCard.tsx` | a comment, "Instagram Story (9:16)" — an aspect ratio for a share image |
| `supabase/functions/legal/index.ts` | the published privacy page stating "No social media SDKs (Facebook, TikTok, etc.)" |

| `facebook.com/v18.0`, `twitter.com/example`, `youtube.com/embed` | dependency URLs and placeholders |

**So: if CAPS has social profiles, they exist only outside this repository — in Roye's head or in
an account nobody wrote down.** Nothing here records a platform, a handle, who created it, what was
posted or when. That question can only be answered by someone with the logins.

⚠️ **One thing to carry into any profile that gets created:** the privacy policy currently
published at `/privacy.html` says the app carries no Facebook or TikTok SDKs. That stays true only
if new profiles are not wired back into the app with a pixel or SDK.

## 2 · The landing page — `caps.ftable.co.il` is the app, not a landing page

Confirmed from the Vercel project rather than assumed: `caps.ftable.co.il` is a domain on the
project **`caps-poker-web`**, whose `vercel.json` builds `expo export --platform web` and rewrites
**`/(.*)` → `/index.html`**. Every path serves the game. The only two routes that are not the app
are `/privacy.html` and `/terms.html`.

There is no marketing page, no hero, no copy — a visitor lands in a poker table.

**Can it carry a video section?** Yes, but not for free: the catch-all rewrite has to be carved
open for a real route, or the videos go on a separate Vercel project. The second is cleaner and
does not risk the app's routing.

🔴 **And the deploy is not what it looks like.** The project *is* git-linked to
`royea-beep/caps-poker`, so a push should deploy — but **the git-triggered builds are failing.**
Every branch deployment today is `state: ERROR`. The production deployments that are `READY` carry
`gitRootDirectory: "dist"` and `gitDirty: 1`, which is the signature of someone running the CLI by
hand from the `dist/` folder. So the live site is updated manually and pushing does not reliably
publish. Anyone planning to add a page should know that before they plan around a push.

## 3 · Claims — the pro-quote problem was fixed in source, and two committed bundles still carry it

**Fixed, and properly.** Commit `9a3acae` (2026-08-21 10:26:30 +0300) archived `proQuotes.ts`,
which had carried **20 fabricated product endorsements from ten living pros, each with an
ElevenLabs voice clone of that person**, plus 82 invented aphorisms credited to 27 more, several of
them dead, none with a citation. The replacement `constants/capsQuotes.ts` attributes every line to
"CAPS Poker" and names nobody else. The reasoning recorded there is the right one — the line is
provenance, not truthfulness.

🔴 **But the built bundles committed to this repo still contain the names**, and they were
committed **ten minutes before the fix**:

| bundle | committed | Negreanu | Hellmuth | Ivey | Brunson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `web-dist/…/index-87fabe1b….js` | `8cb1eaf` 10:16:57 | 7 | 7 | 9 | 5 |
| `dist-web/…/index-1fe8d414….js` | `8cb1eaf` 10:16:57 | 7 | 7 | 9 | 5 |
| `dist/…/index-a57f3a87….js` | (gitignored) | **0** | **0** | **0** | **0** |

`web-dist/` is tracked by git; `dist/` is not. The production deployments are made from `dist/`,
which is clean, so the live site is **probably** fine — **but this session could not verify it**,
because `ftable.co.il` is denied at this container's proxy. One fetch settles it:

```bash
curl -s https://caps.ftable.co.il/ | grep -o 'index-[a-f0-9]*\.js'   # then grep that bundle for Negreanu
```

Until someone runs it, "the live site is clean" is an inference from deployment metadata, not a
fact. The two stale bundles should be deleted from the repo regardless — a fabricated endorsement
sitting in a tracked file is one `outputDirectory` change away from being served again.

**Other published claims worth a second look**, none of them fabricated but two of them soft:

- *"No ads. No internet required."* (`APPSTORE_METADATA.md`) — the app talks to Supabase for bug
  reports and `app_config`. True of gameplay, not of the app.
- **Two different store descriptions exist and they disagree.** `store/app-store-description.txt`
  sells "Solo play vs AI · 3D floating cards · Daily rewards and win streaks";
  `APPSTORE_METADATA.md` sells "local WiFi multiplayer via room code · No ads · No internet
  required". Same listing, two sources of truth, and nothing says which one shipped.
- *"No real money. No gambling. Virtual chips only."* — consistent with the 18+ simulated-gambling
  rating in the same file. No problem.
