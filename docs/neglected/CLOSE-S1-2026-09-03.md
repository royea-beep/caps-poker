### ⚠️ `verify_jwt` — restated where it will be seen
`verify-purchase` runs with **`verify_jwt = true` today, and that is currently doing real work.**
When PayPlus is wired, the provider's webhook cannot present a user JWT, so `verify_jwt` **must come
off** — and at that moment **the payload signature becomes the only gate on crediting chips.** If
the signature check is weak, absent, or not constant-time, an unauthenticated caller credits
themselves real money's worth of chips. Do not remove `verify_jwt` in the same change that adds the
signature check; add and prove the signature first. *(Also recorded in the migration header and in
the handoff.)*

### The ledger sense — and the metric that never meant this
After the fix the mint is closed at the **entry point**: no session, no credit, so no
`chip_transactions` row and no `leaderboard` move. **`gap = 0` was never evidence of any of this** —
it is green precisely *because* the mints wrote both sides, ledger and balance, in one call. It
measures internal consistency and nothing else. Do not quote it as mint resistance again.
