## 6 · The two filed items

### The splash is the previous identity — and it is visible

Filed as "maroon vs near-black". Looking at it, it is worse and more interesting than that.

`assets/splash.png` is **1284×2778 and dark green** — corners `#08341A` — carrying a
**sans-serif yellow** CAPS wordmark over felt. `app.json` declares `backgroundColor: "#1C0508"`,
a dark maroon. The app it launches into is `#0a0a0a` near-black with a **serif brass** wordmark.

So the first second is **three grounds and two different wordmarks**:

| | colour | wordmark |
|---|---|---|
| `backgroundColor` bars | `#1C0508` maroon | — |
| `splash.png` | `#08341A` green | sans-serif, bright yellow |
| the app | `#0a0a0a` near-black | serif, brass `#c9a84c` |

**Is the maroon visible in practice? Yes, on effectively every device.** `resizeMode: contain`
with a 0.4622-aspect image letterboxes or pillarboxes everywhere — even the 14 Pro Max (0.461)
gets bars, and an SE (0.563) gets wide side bars.

**But the maroon is the smaller problem.** The splash is the *previous visual identity*, and it
now contradicts the hero we just shipped — a green, sans-serif splash opening a black, serif app.

**Cost, honestly split.** The `backgroundColor` line is a two-minute change. The image is not —
it is a design decision of the same kind D1 was, and I have not made it. **I recommend against
changing the background alone**: green art on black bars is arguably more jarring than green art
on maroon bars. The two should move together. I can render a D1-idiom splash in about twenty
minutes if Roye orders it; **it is the actual first frame, before any hero**, so I would order it
soon.

### `cups.tier` — a two-line fix, and a mild symptom

`app/(tabs)/cups.tsx:9` declares `TIER_LABELS: Record<string, string>` keyed
`bronze…diamond`, but `cups.tier` is an **integer** 1–5 in the database. So `TIER_LABELS[cup.tier]`
is always `undefined` and both call sites (`:37` accessibility label, `:47` visible name) fall
through to `cup.name_he`.

**The symptom is mild**, which is why it survived: `name_he` holds `"Bronze Cup"`, so rows read
*"Bronze Cup"* instead of *"Bronze"* — redundant next to a trophy icon, not broken.

**The fix is two lines in one file**: index by `cup.id`, which already *is* `'bronze' | 'silver' |
'gold' | 'platinum' | 'diamond'` and is the natural key, rather than by `tier`. Not done — it is
not in this sprint's scope and Roye should order it.
