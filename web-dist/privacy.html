CAPS Poker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Overview

CAPS Poker is a card game that runs entirely on your device. We respect your privacy and have designed the app to minimize data collection.

Data We Collect

None. CAPS Poker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All game data (chip balance, settings, game history) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage.

Local Multiplayer

When using local WiFi multiplayer, your device communicates directly with other devices on the same local network. No data is sent to any external server. Player names entered during multiplayer sessions are not stored after the session ends.

Analytics & Tracking

CAPS Poker does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. No third-party SDKs collect data from this app.

Internet Access

CAPS Poker does not require an internet connection. The app functions fully offline. Local multiplayer uses your local WiFi network only.

Children's Privacy

CAPS Poker does not collect any data from any users, including children. The app contains simulated gambling mechanics (virtual chips with no real-money value) and is rated 17+.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit caps.ftable.co.il.
