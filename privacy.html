Privacy Policy — CAPS Poker

Last updated 8 September 2026 · applies to the CAPS Poker app and to caps.ftable.co.il

CAPS Poker is a free card game played with virtual chips only. There is no real-money gambling, no cash prizes, no payouts, and nothing you win inside the game can be cashed out or exchanged for money or anything of value.

This page describes what the app actually stores and who actually receives it. It was written by reading the code, not from a template.

Who you are to us

You can play without giving us anything. When you first open CAPS, the app creates an anonymous device identifier — a random string — and an anonymous account on our backend. That identifier is how your chips and progress find their way back to you. It is not your name, your email or your phone number, and we do not attempt to link it to them.

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose it, Google tells us the basic profile information tied to that sign-in so your progress can follow you to another device. If you never sign in, we never receive it. Almost everybody who plays CAPS today is anonymous.

What we store

  • Game progress — your chip balance, hands played, hands won, your board-by-board results, your win streak and your daily-login streak.
  • Achievements — which of the in-game achievements you have earned, and when.
  • Leaderboard standing — your chip total and player name as shown on the in-game leaderboard.
  • Analytics events — which screens you opened and which actions you took, tied to the device identifier, so we can find where the game confuses people.
  • Push notification token — only if you allow notifications. It lets us send a reminder to that device and nothing else.
  • Bug reports — only when you send one. See the next section; it carries more than the words you type.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photo library, your phone number, or any payment or financial information.

⚠️ What a bug report actually contains

If you tap the report button and send us a problem, the report carries more than your description, and you should know that before you send one. Attached automatically:

  • the screen you were on and the text you wrote;
  • a breadcrumb trail — the screens you visited before it, in order;
  • the last 20 lines of the app's own console output, which can include values the game was working with at the time;
  • your device identifier, platform, operating-system version, app version, installed build number and the language the app was set to;
  • a screenshot, and an audio clip, if you chose to attach them.

Please do not type anything into a bug report that you would not want us to read.

Who else receives your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We do use other companies to run the game, and these are all of them:

  • Supabase — hosts the database, the anonymous accounts, uploaded files and the server functions. Effectively everything above is stored here.
  • Vercel — hosts caps.ftable.co.il, the browser version of the game.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Telegram — when you send a bug report, it is forwarded automatically to a private channel our team reads, including the screenshot if you attached one.
  • Anthropic — a bug report's text and technical details are sent to the Claude API, which summarises it so we can prioritise it. This is automatic and happens for every report.
  • OpenAI — only if you attach an audio clip to a bug report; it is transcribed into text so we can read it.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications, if you allowed them.
  • Apple — distributes the app. No purchase or payment information reaches us, because the game currently sells nothing for money.

Money

Chips in CAPS are a score. They are earned by playing, by daily rewards and by streaks, and they can be spent inside the game on cosmetic items such as card backs and avatars. Chips cannot be bought with money and cannot be converted into money. The app has no active in-app purchases and has never processed a payment. If that ever changes, this page will say so before it does.

⚠️ Deleting your data

Ask us by email and we will delete it manually. Write to caps@ftable.co.il from the device you play on, or include your player name, and we will remove your account and its data.

We are telling you this rather than pointing at a button because the in-app Delete account control does not currently work. It was switched off after we found it could be used to delete somebody else's account, and it has not yet been replaced. The button is still visible in Settings and will tell you the same thing if you press it. Nothing is deleted when you press it.

We do not currently offer an automated way to export a copy of your data. If you want one, ask at the same address and we will put it together by hand.

How long we keep it

Game progress is kept for as long as the account exists, so that your chips are still there when you come back. Bug reports and analytics events are kept while they are still useful for fixing and improving the game. Ask us to delete your data and we remove it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (TLS) and the database uses row-level security so that one player's rows are not readable by another. No system is perfect; if we discover a breach that affects you, we will say so.

Age

CAPS Poker simulates gambling with virtual chips and is intended for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has been playing and has given us data, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else about this policy: caps@ftable.co.il
