# CAPS Poker — Privacy Policy

CAPS Poker does not collect personal information.

**What we store locally on your device:**
- Game progress and chip balance (via AsyncStorage)
- Settings and preferences

**Bug reports (only when you choose to submit):**
- Screenshot of the game
- Audio recording (only if you speak)
- Device model and iOS version
- App version

This data is used only to fix bugs and is not shared with third parties.

**We do NOT:**
- Require an account
- Use real money
- Show ads
- Track you across apps
- Share your data
