CAPS Poker

=== SUBTITLE (30 chars) ===
Omaha on multiple boards at once

=== DESCRIPTION ===
CAPS is a new way to play poker.

Play 2, 3, or 4 boards at the same time.
Each board has its own pot. Win a board, take the chips.
Win ALL boards — you get a COMPLETE bonus: 50% extra.

It's Omaha poker. Reimagined.

HOW TO PLAY:
You get 4 cards per board. Arrange them. Community cards are revealed automatically. Best Omaha hand wins each board.

FEATURES:
• Solo play vs AI
• 2, 3, or 4 player modes
• 3D floating cards
• Daily rewards and win streaks
• Full Omaha hand evaluation
• No real money. No gambling. Virtual chips only.
